Personal Secret System

8PSS

Your identity is not your password.

8PSS explores a private identity system where encrypted secrets belong to the person and can be presented when identity needs to be proven.

8

01 / Model

Begin with the person.

Person ↓ Identity ↓ Secrets ↓ Proof ↓ Access

the old abstraction

site→username→password

02 / The system

Three terms. One lifecycle.

P Personal
The system belongs to the person. The encrypted private vault is authoritative.
S Secret
A secret does not have to be a password. It may be generated, captured, imported, derived or cryptographically created.
S System
Generation, encryption, authorization, storage, retrieval, Autofill and deletion are parts of one lifecycle.

03 / Infinity lifecycle

The vault is a relationship, not a destination.

04 / Principle

8PSS is a Personal Secret System in which encrypted credentials belong to identities, rather than identities being reduced to passwords.

05 / Origin

From Magic 8 to 8PSS.

Magic 8 began by turning local randomness into memorable passphrases.

8PSS extends the idea: generate the secret, keep it private, associate it with identity, and present it only when required.

Current Magic 8 web project

Local passphrase and password generation, with an optional themed fortune. Secrets are generated in the browser.

Concept and direction

Encrypted local credential storage, biometric authorization, Android Autofill, identity-associated secrets, TOTP material, passkeys and cryptographic credentials where appropriate, and deliberate deletion.

Items described as direction are not claims of completed or independently verified functionality.
